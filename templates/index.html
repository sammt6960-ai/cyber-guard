    STAY PROTECTED

    Security essentials

    Simple habits can reduce your exposure to online threats.

    🔗

    Inspect links

    Check the spelling of the domain and be cautious of unexpected login or payment requests.

    🔐

    Use MFA

    Enable multifactor authentication and use unique, strong passwords for important accounts.

    🎣

    Spot phishing

    Verify urgent requests through a separate trusted channel instead of following the message link.

    ⬆️

    Update devices

    Install security updates for your operating system, browser, and applications.
